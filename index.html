Roser Batlle Roca

PhD Candidate on Transparency & Ethics of Generative AI in Music
Music Technology Group · Universitat Pompeu Fabra

My research focuses on making music-generative AI more transparent and accountable, through documentation frameworks that assess model openness, methods to detect potential training data replication, and perceptual studies with expert listeners. I aim to contribute to the responsible and open development of these systems, with attention to their ethical, social, legal and economic implications. I work in collaboration with Sony and the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre.
